Help & Info

Privacy & Processing
  • Client-Side Only: Everything stays entirely in your browser. No images are uploaded to any server.
  • Clipboard: Supports pasting via Ctrl+V and copying annotated images directly.
Features & Tools
  • Paint: Freehand drawing tool with custom sizing and color swatches.
  • Arrow & Line: Draw straight indicator lines or crisp open arrowheads.
  • Box: Draw precise rectangular outlines.
  • Censor: Pixelate and block out sensitive information securely using average-colour blocks.
  • Text: Place text on canvas, drag to reposition, then click Place or press Enter.
