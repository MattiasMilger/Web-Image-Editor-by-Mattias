Help & Info

Privacy & Processing
  • Client-Side Only: Everything stays entirely in your browser. No images are uploaded to any server.
  • Clipboard: Supports pasting via Ctrl+V and copying annotated images directly.
Features & Tools
  • Paint: Freehand drawing tool with custom sizing and color swatches.
  • Arrow & Line: Draw straight indicator lines or clean open-headed arrows. Arrows end exactly where you release and stay inside the image, so they never clip.
  • Box & Circle: Draw precise rectangular or circular shapes. Use the Shape option to choose outline or filled.
  • Censor: Pixelate and block out sensitive information securely using average-colour blocks. The Size setting sets the block size.
  • Text: Click the image and type. Press Enter for a new line. Use Size to make the text larger or smaller. Drag the handles on the left and right side to change the width of the text box (the text wraps to fit) and the handles on the top and bottom to stretch the text taller or flatter. The corner handles do both, so the box can be any rectangle or square. Drag the text to move it, then click anywhere outside it (or press Ctrl+Enter) to place it. Esc cancels.
  • Crop: Pick the Crop tool and a box appears on the image. Drag its handles to choose the part to keep, drag inside it to move it, or drag outside it to draw a new box. Everything outside the box is dimmed. Click Crop (or press Enter) to cut the image down to the box, or Cancel (Esc) to leave it as it is. Undo takes it back.
  • Download: The Download button next to the Image menu saves the image as PNG or JPG.
  • Canvas size: In the Image menu. Like Canvas Size in GIMP. Set a new width and height, choose where the current picture sits (the 3×3 grid) and what colour the new area gets. A smaller size crops. Undo takes it back.
  • Zoom: Use the Zoom buttons, Ctrl/Cmd + mouse wheel (or trackpad pinch), or the + / − / 0 keys. Pan by scrolling, holding Space and dragging, or middle-mouse dragging. On touch screens use two fingers to pan and pinch.
  • Blank image: Start from a blank canvas of any size with a colour (white by default) or transparent background, then draw, insert images and add text on it.
  • Transparent colour: The checkerboard swatch erases to transparency with any tool. Download PNG to keep it (JPG fills transparent areas with white).
  • Insert image: With an image open, drop, paste (Ctrl+V) or use Insert image to add another picture on top. Drag it to move and drag the handles to resize (hold Shift on a corner to keep proportions). Click anywhere outside it to place it, or press Esc to cancel.
  • Eyedropper: In the Color panel, click the pipette icon, then click the image to copy that pixel’s colour into the selected swatch.
Appearance
  • Dark / Light mode: Click the ◐ button to switch themes. Dark is the default, and your choice is remembered on this device.
